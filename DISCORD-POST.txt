Alright yall, Lightweight Weather & Skies 1.0 is here 🌩️

I started this because I wanted clouds worth looking up at and apparently I don't know when to leave the sky alone lmao. They move and morph, storms roll across the map, and there's rain, snow, forked lightning, anvil crawlers, thunder, day/night skies, moon phases, auroras, meteors and regional weather. The world sounds different too.

I made it to make VR, first-person and third-person play feel more immersive—the weather should feel like it's happening around you, not just pasted over the screen.

This is the first release. Gen 1 with Battle Art is the main track on PC and standalone Quest. Gen 2 on Quest is beta and non-VR Android is alpha, so send me clips if something acts up. It's one weather ZIP; Battle Art and the Quest bridge are separate installs. Back up your save and don't run another weather mod alongside it.

Showcase Controls stayed. If you see MAKE IT SPICY, give the pepper a chance 🌶️😂

If you're making a voxel mod and want compatibility, reach out. I'm happy to work with anyone on it.
