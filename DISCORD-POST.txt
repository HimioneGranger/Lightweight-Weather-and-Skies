Alright yall Lightweight Weather & Skies 🌩️

This started with me just wanting some good clouds and apparently i don't know when to leave the sky alone lmao

Clouds that move and morph, storms that travel across the map, forked lightning, anvil crawlers, lightning rolling through the clouds, rain, snow, auroras and meteor showers. A lunar cycle and regional weather too. I wanted a sky that makes you stop and look up.

Works with Battle Art on Gen1Recomp right now. PC and standalone Quest support, with more compatibility to come. You still need the compatible host/bridge—this is the weather mod, not the whole game.

And yes MAKE IT SPICY stayed 😂 Everything for grabbing clips is in Showcase Controls so you don't have to wait around for the perfect storm.

Anime cries aren't bundled but i recommend Dynamic Cries:
