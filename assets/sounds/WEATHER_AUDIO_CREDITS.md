# Weather sound credits

These replacements are separate from the upstream weather code license.

- **rain_var1–4.ogg**: Ylmir, [Rain (loopable)](https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Four supplied rain samples.
- **rain_soft.ogg**: barkenov, [Soft rain.WAV](https://freesound.org/people/barkenov/sounds/640655/), CC0 1.0. Obtained as a publicly credited re-encode from [Rain Sounds for Sleeping](https://rainsoundsforsleeping.com/licensing/), not the original WAV master.
- **wind_var1–3.ogg**: Luke.RUSTLTD, [wind1](https://opengameart.org/content/wind1), CC0 1.0. Three distinct synthesized wind beds, not field recordings. The author credits Andy Farnell's PureData tutorial.
- **thunder_var1–8.ogg**: Gregor Quendel, [Thunder / Lightning Ambience – Field Recording](https://opengameart.org/content/thunder-lightning-ambience-field-recording), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Eight separated excerpts from one recording session, not eight independent recordings. Adapted by the Lightweight Weather & Skies project: excerpted, high-pass filtered, loudness adjusted, faded and converted to mono 24 kHz Ogg Vorbis. No endorsement by the original author is implied.

All files were high-pass filtered at 35 Hz, loudness adjusted, given edge fades and encoded as mono 24 kHz Ogg Vorbis. Rain and wind are crossfaded in-game. Thunder remains event-driven. Source and output hashes, excerpt positions and conversion measurements are in `release-review/AUDIO-SOURCES.json`.

License pages were checked on September 7, 2026. CC0 and CC BY apply only to the identified recordings; they do not relicense the complete mod.
