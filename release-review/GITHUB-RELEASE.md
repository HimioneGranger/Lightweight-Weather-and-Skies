# Lightweight Weather & Skies 1.0.0

The first public release brings moving, morphing clouds and changing weather to Battle Art, especially for VR, first-person and third-person play. Gen 1 on PC and standalone Quest is the main track; Gen 2 Quest support is beta and non-VR Android support is alpha.

## Highlights

- Storms travel across outdoor areas, with rain, snow, wind, varied lightning, delayed thunder and gradual clearing.
- The day/night sky includes moon phases, auroras, meteor showers and regional weather patterns.
- Showcase Controls offer opt-in previews for recording clips. Return **WEATHER → AUTO** after a preview that selects manual weather.
- [The README](https://github.com/HimioneGranger/Lightweight-Weather-and-Skies/tree/v1.0.0#readme) has labeled cloud, storm, lunar and lightning GIFs, plus setup details.

## Install

1. Back up your save and disable any older LWS version or other weather mod.
2. Import the attached `Lightweight-Weather-and-Skies-1.0.0.zip`, enable it, and restart.
3. Install compatible Battle Art separately. Standalone Quest also requires its compatible Quest bridge; neither is bundled in this ZIP.

Gen 1 PC was checked by the owner on Gen1Recomp 0.2.24 with Battle Art 1.11.0; the cloud, sun, star and rain comparisons passed. Gen 1 Quest visual/audio checks passed in a private RC Lab, while the final cloud-spacing adjustment was checked on PC and has not had a separate Quest visual pass. Gen 2 remains beta; its performance was **not measured** for this release. Non-VR Android remains alpha after earlier S24 compatibility reports. PCVR and unrelated renderers are not claimed. “Lightweight” describes the design goal, not a zero-cost guarantee.

For bugs, please include your platform, host/Battle Art version, Quest bridge status, mod list and a short clip. Report slow dialogue or map entry separately from missing visuals.
